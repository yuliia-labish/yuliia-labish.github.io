Flamingo New Homes

A marketplace of new-build homes straight from developers, with cabinets for developers and buyers and an admin panel for the Flamingo team.

The task

Buying a new-build home without a middleman

Flamingo New Homes is a marketplace of new-build residential and commercial property sold directly by developers. The product was designed from scratch and is now live.

It had to work for three groups of people at once:

  • buyers, who search and compare complexes across cities;
  • developers, who publish their complexes and keep them up to date;
  • the Flamingo team, who look after developers, listings, users and payments.

How I worked

From the first design to the main flows

Another designer started the project: set the visual style and ran the competitor analysis and research. I then took over and became the main designer for all key pages and flows.

  1. Took over from the first designer and kept the visual style already in place.
  2. Maintained the design system: filled it in and added new components with their states.
  3. Regular discussions with the client about problems, ideas and priorities.
  4. Decisions based on the earlier competitor analysis.
  5. Designed the buyer website on desktop and mobile.
  6. Designed the developer and buyer cabinets and the admin panel for the Flamingo team.

For buyers

Finding the right complex

Search, map and filters built around how people look for a new home: by place, type and price per m².

Search on the first screen

Location, property type and price per m² right in the hero, with quick ways into the satellite view and into drawing your own search area.

One switch for list, map and satellite

The same results and filters in three views. Prices per m² show in hryvnias and dollars, and every complex has favourites, share and compare.

Complexes drawn as real buildings

On the map each complex is its real building outline with the price per m². On a phone you can also draw your own search area.

Filters for every property type

Eight property types, from flats to parking spaces. Filters cover price per m², area, ceiling height, construction status, completion date and financing: instalments, credit, mortgage and promotions.

Everything about a complex on one page

A sales office with hours and contacts, a gallery with video and deal badges, and tabs for infrastructure, layouts and prices, price dynamics, promotions, financing, construction progress and documents.

For developers and the Flamingo team

Running the marketplace

Behind the website: a cabinet where developers publish and manage their complexes, and an admin panel for the Flamingo team.

Adding a complex step by step

A developer fills in a complex tab by tab: plans, about, layouts, price dynamics, promotions and discounts, financing, construction progress, documents and contacts.

Statuses you can read in a long table

Separate coloured chips for the subscription and for the complex, so problems stand out in a long list. Tab filters (all, active, pending, drafts, inactive), search and an actions menu in every row.

Analytics for the team and for each developer

KPIs for a period, a traffic chart with details on hover, and breakdowns by age, gender, traffic source and country. Developers get the same view for their own complexes.

Payments and plans

Payment history with paid and unpaid filters and clear statuses, plus the edge cases: changing the payment method, attaching a document, resending an invoice and cancelling a payment.

Status

Released and live

The marketplace is live with real complexes from developers in Lviv, Kyiv, Ivano-Frankivsk, Uzhhorod and other cities.
